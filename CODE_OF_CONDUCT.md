# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This is a small, focused open source project. Interactions here should be:

- Respectful and constructive
- Focused on the work — bugs, features, improvements
- Welcoming to contributors of all experience levels

## Not Acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Dismissing contributions without explanation
- Off-topic noise

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
communication under the pimlabs/brewmaster repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, or contributions that
do not align with this standard. Repeated violations result in a ban from the project.
